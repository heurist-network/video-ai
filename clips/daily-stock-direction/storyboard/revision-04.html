DAILY STOCK PREDICTIONS / STORYBOARD 04

Three markets. Your call.

A social promo about daily stock predictions. Prices lead; everything else supports them. Option one · 8 seconds · 16:9 · planned 1920 × 1080 at 30 fps. Three rendered style frames. Screen mesh, narrow margins and a subtle left-nearer tilt. Composed promotional graphics, not a product interface.

PRODUCTION DIRECTION

01

Meet today’s markets

0.0–1.6s · frames 0–47
Composition

Three simple blocks: AAPL largest, TSLA and NVDA beside it. Only ticker, oversized price and small signed dollar change. No company names, badges, dates, percentages or charts.

Motion

0.00–0.25s: AAPL enters; TSLA follows at 0.20s and NVDA at 0.40s. All settle by 0.65s. Hold to 1.60s. Keep the same three blocks into shot 02.

Source / boundary

Real Yahoo snapshot values, presented as promotional typography. Data timestamps and calculation details are in the source notes below, outside the film.

02

Price takes the spotlight

1.6–5.0s · frames 48–149
Composition

One continuous beat combines price emphasis and the widening AAPL block. Whole dollars remain steady while the two decimal digits roll vertically like an odometer, then settle. The small signed dollar changes stay fixed.

Motion

1.60–2.20s: widen AAPL and enlarge its price together. 1.80–3.20s: decimal reels rotate with an 80ms offset between digits and a 140ms offset between cards. Decelerate into the saved prices. Hold 3.20–5.00s, then carry the same surfaces into the closing shot.

Source / boundary

Rolling digits are a typographic transition, not observed market ticks. Settle to AAPL $334.45, TSLA $360.84 and NVDA $212.29. The produced video implements both the decimal roll and the surface transition.

03

Make your call

5.0–8.0s · frames 150–239
Composition

Keep the oversized AAPL price. Add a much larger “UP or DOWN?” beneath it as the closing prediction hook, with no buttons, cursor or interface controls.

Motion

5.00–5.50s: introduce the short hook below the price; keep the price dominant. Hold 5.50–8.00s. End on the intact composition.

Source / boundary

The hook refers to daily direction versus the previous regular-session close. Neither prediction is selected or presented as a result. Source date and time remain in the review notes.
